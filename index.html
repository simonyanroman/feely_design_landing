Waves & Convex 80×80: Silicone-Free Parametric Gypsum Tiles

Traditional silicone molds make R&D slow and expensive. We engineered a unified outer retention frame with interchangeable 3D-printed parametric faceplates—cutting screen-to-cast time from days to hours.

Innovation Direct FDM formwork with draft-angle compensation and reusable clamping rails instead of silicone.
Geometry Grasshopper wave & spherical attractor scripts that lock perimeter edges flat for seamless diptych/wall mounting.
Colorways Raw unbleached gypsum & deep iron-oxide terracotta.

CASE_02 // HYBRID MATERIAL R&D
MATERIALS: Crazy Horse Leather + Steel + Polymer CAD: Rhino 3D
Composite Product Leathercraft Tooling

Tension Leather Clock & Embossing Tooling

An exploration of material contrast: a radial cut Crazy Horse leather dial suspended under tension inside a minimalist steel ring, backed by a custom 3D-printed structural movement housing.

Challenge Achieve crisp geometric tension and tactile embossing on pull-up leather without costly brass dies.
Solution Parametric radial tab geometry + high-density polymer cold-press stamps and precision rear housing.
Result 5–7x tooling cost reduction and rapid visual/tactile prototyping in Rhino & Blender.

0.8mm Extrusion Differential Growth Tactile Objects

Non-Planar Overhangs, Spiral Vessels & Analog Accessories

Pushing FDM hardware with 0.8mm nozzles, 0.6mm layer heights, and custom Grasshopper toolpaths to produce structural ribbed textures, differential growth vessels, and tactile desk stands.

Parameters 0.8mm nozzle // 1.2mm wall // 15 mm/s controlled extrusion // zero support material.
Portfolio Ribbed Instax film stands, Differential Growth vases, Perlin noise planters, and parametric threads.
