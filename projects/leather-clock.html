Suspended Leather Dial

Instead of gluing leather flat onto a rigid board, the clock face uses 8 radial tabs that wrap around a perimeter metal hoop. The rear polymer disc locks the tension and houses the quartz movement, keeping the leather taut like a drumhead while celebrating its raw edges.

CAD File: Engineered in Rhino 3D with custom surface deformation algorithms.
[02 // LEATHERCRAFT TOOLING]

High-Density Polymer Dies

Machining brass or magnesium dies is prohibitively expensive for short runs or testing tactile depth on Crazy Horse and vegetable-tanned leathers. We print solid-infill polymer matrices with chamfered groove profiles that withstand cold-press tonnage without delaminating.

Turnaround: 24–48 hours from vector/CAD to physical press test.
