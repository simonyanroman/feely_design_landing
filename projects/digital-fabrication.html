Thick-Layer Spiral Protocol

To achieve glass-like translucency and structural rigidity in a single continuous wall, we bypass standard 0.4mm profiles in favor of heavy extrusion calibrated on direct-drive hardware:

Nozzle / Layer: 0.8 mm nozzle // 0.6 mm layer height
Outer Wall: 1.2 mm continuous spiral vase mode
Speed & Cooling: 15 mm/s controlled flow; bridging slowdown disabled
