Why We Replaced Silicone

Classical casting requires printing a master model, post-processing it, building a containment box, mixing expensive two-part platinum silicone, degassing, and waiting 24 hours before pouring a single test tile. For parametric design—where every wave frequency or dome curvature is an experiment—silicone kills momentum.

Cost Reduction: ~75% lower R&D cost per new texture variant.
Lead Time: Screen to first gypsum cast in under 4 hours.
[02 // MODULAR ARCHITECTURE]

Unified Outer Frame + Swap-In Faceplates

All tiles within a format family (such as our 80×80 mm series) share the exact same outer perimeter rails and corner fasteners. To test a new pattern—moving from spherical Convex_80 to directional Waves—we only print the thin relief faceplate and snap it into the frame.

Boundary Lock: Grasshopper scripts enforce a flat perimeter datum so tiles align cleanly in diptychs and triptychs.
[03 // COMMISSIONING]

Available for Studios & Brands

We supply both finished architectural gypsum tile compositions (in raw unbleached white or custom mineral pigments like terracotta) and custom-engineered 3D-printed mold systems for your own workshop.
